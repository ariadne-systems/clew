**Title**
A stakeholder needs specs and their code links to be cheap to author and keep current

**Lens**: STK

**Description**
A developer needs to create specs and bind code to them cheaply, so traceability is sustained through the life of the project rather than abandoned the first time it becomes a chore.

**Rationale**
Traceability schemes fail in practice not because they are wrong but because they are tedious; if authoring and linking cost too much, they stop happening and the data rots.
Keeping the cost low is what makes the discipline survivable.

**Verification Description**
Satisfied when allocating ids, authoring and finalizing specs, and reporting failures are quick and unambiguous — realized through the spec lifecycle (SYS-005) and diagnostic clarity (SYS-006).
